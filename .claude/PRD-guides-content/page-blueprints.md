# Page blueprints

The outline each guide is written to. H2s are questions people type (Singapore autocomplete, `research.md` §2). The ledes are drafts at the 40–60 word band, already claim-safe. Everything else is direction for the writer, not final copy. Sources are numbered as they would appear in the page's `## Sources`.

**Wanted** marks a fact we want but must not invent: it comes from the fit report, staff or the owner.

**Sections**

1. **Can your pillow cause neck pain?** Yes, it can contribute. Checklist "Signs your pillow is part of the problem":
   - you wake stiff and it eases by midday;
   - you fold or stack your pillow;
   - your ear or shoulder goes numb;
   - the pillow has flattened.

   Sources: Mayo Clinic causes [1]; Gordon 2010, "own pillows did not guarantee symptom-free waking" [2]. Visual: the checklist as a short list with icons.
2. **What pillow height do you need for your sleep position?** Open with: there is no single right height; it depends on how you sleep and your build [3 Lei 2021]. Visual: a table, rows Side / Back / Stomach / Combination; columns "What the pillow must do", "What sets the height", "Common mistake". Side: height tracks shoulder width [4 Tian 2025]. Back: lower, supports the neck curve. Stomach: lowest or none, and HealthHub advises against it [5]. Combination: a shape with two heights. Image: `diff-body-back-sleep.webp`. **Wanted:** "In our fittings, side sleepers most often needed size …" (fit report).
3. **How firm should a pillow for neck pain be, and which filling?** Firm enough to hold its height through the night. Visual: a materials table with memory foam, latex, feather/down, polyester fibre, buckwheat and modular fibre clusters as rows; columns "Holds height", "Can you adjust it", "Washable", "Sleeps cool in humid weather". Evidence: feather did worst in trials, latex best in one series, and a 2025 review found no clear winner [2, 6 Ghosh 2025]. Note: buckwheat dust can trigger allergies.
4. **Do cervical or contour pillows work better?** Short answer: shape alone has not beaten a well-fitted regular pillow [7 Gordon 2009]; height and fit matter more. Link: the cervical pillow guide.
5. **What does the research say about pillows and neck pain?** Five plain bullets:
   - shape and height change neck alignment [8 Pang 2021];
   - no material is proven best [6];
   - pillows have not been shown to improve sleep quality [6, 8];
   - adjusting height to the person was followed by less pain in one uncontrolled study [9 Yamada 2023];
   - higher is not better [10, worded gently].

   This section carries the old systematic-review URL's intent. Callout: "What a pillow can and can't do."
6. **Off-the-shelf, adjustable or fitted: which suits you?** Table: three options × six rows: who sets the height; fits your shoulder width; can change later; try before you take it home; washable; where to buy. Then the decision-point CTA: "Not sure of your height? Measure at home online, or get fitted at our Singapore studio."

   1. Measure: shoulder length for Basic, four measurements for Premium.
   2. Our team sets the size and filling mix.

   Facts: patented M shape supports back and side sleeping in one pillow; modular, washable components; 10-year warranty on filling height. Images: `shoulderlength_800x.webp`, `sizecomparison.webp`. Video: Dr Steven Hulme's review (`nvFI6woJD9g`), with a one-line summary in text. Check his registration and how to title him (SCAP; D3).

**Sections**

1. **What is a cervical pillow?** "Cervical" means the seven neck vertebrae, which curve inward [1 StatPearls]. Types table, rows: contour/wave foam, cervical roll, butterfly, water-based, adjustable fill, fitted modular; columns "How it supports", "Suits", "Watch out for". Visual: **new** pillow-types illustration.
2. **Do cervical pillows help neck pain?** Answer straight:
   - shape and height change alignment [2 Pang 2021];
   - a contour shape alone showed no advantage over a regular pillow [3 Gordon 2009];
   - early reviews found too little evidence [4 Shields 2006].

   Quote the Singapore view: SKH's principal physiotherapist says there is "no strong evidence that specific cervical pillows… directly reduce or prevent neck pain" [5]. Then: what matters is fit. Honesty here is the page's edge over listicles.
3. **Who is a cervical pillow good for, and who should skip it?**
   - Suits: back and side sleepers with a more curved neck or rounder head; people who like firm neck contact.
   - Skip or go lower: stomach sleepers; people who move a lot at night; anyone whose neck feels pushed forward.

   Image: `round-flat-head-comp.png` with the caption "Not needed for a flatter head; helpful for a rounder head and curved neck."
4. **How do you choose the right cervical pillow?** A five-step checklist:
   1. height for your shoulder width and sleep position;
   2. neck-roll height;
   3. firmness that holds;
   4. washable and cool in Singapore humidity;
   5. a trial or adjustment policy.

   Image: `NeckDepth_800x.webp` (how neck depth is measured).
5. **Which side of a contour pillow should you use?** The higher edge for side sleeping and broader shoulders; the lower edge for back sleeping. Test it: your nose should line up with your breastbone when you lie on your back.
6. **How long does it take to get used to a cervical pillow?** Give the honest range staff see. **Wanted:** staff's typical adjustment period. If it still feels wrong, book a re-adjustment (Premium includes a free visit within its window; link the policy, no numbers in copy).
7. **How does Mpillow's cervical neck support work?** Facts:
   - an insert placed inside a Premium Mpillow, not a separate pillow;
   - staff decide in the fitting whether your head and neck profile needs one;
   - removable, washable and replaceable;
   - it keeps the fillings from flattening at the neck.

   Images: `cns-insert.png`, `cns-contact-point.png`, `mpillow-cervical-neck.webp`. Video: **new** 20–40 s cervical insert clip. Interim: the customisation video (`V2H03lq9IYU`). Then the decision-point CTA.
8. **What customers say**: topic reviews (the cervical filter).
9. **When should you see a doctor?** The shared red-flags callout.
10. **Frequently asked questions** (6):
    - Are cervical pillows good for side sleepers?
    - Can a cervical pillow make neck pain worse?
    - How long does a cervical pillow last?
    - Is an orthopaedic pillow the same as a cervical pillow? Answer: "orthopaedic" is a marketing label, not a standard.
