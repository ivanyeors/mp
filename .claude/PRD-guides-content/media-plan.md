## 2. Videos (slice 08)

**Kit and setup** for all three:
- a phone on a tripod at chest height, shooting 4K/30 in landscape (make vertical cuts in the edit);
- a clip-on mic on the narrator;
- window light from the side, or one soft lamp, with the overhead lights off if they flicker;
- a plain wall behind;
- the branded tape measure in shot.

**Before filming:**
- Get signed consent from anyone who is not staff.
- Keep customer names and screens out of frame.
- Speak slowly, about 150 words a minute.
- Say what you are doing as you do it, so the captions describe the picture.

## 5. Claim wording (D2)

The rule: **neck pain is the reader's problem, never the product's promise.** HSA judges a product by how it is presented (GL-06). Products "generally intended for pain relief" are medical devices; products that "promote good sleep" or "soothe or relax muscle" are not.

| Write | Don't write | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Wake up with a stiff neck? Check your pillow's height first." | "This pillow relieves neck pain." | Naming the problem is fine. Promising relief is a medical purpose. |
| "Keeps your head level with your spine when you sleep on your side." | "Corrects your posture" / "realigns your spine" | "Modification … of the anatomy" is in the device definition. |
| "Fitted to your shoulder width, so your neck isn't bent all night." | "Pain-free sleep" | An improbable-result claim (Google) and a relief claim (HSA). |
| "Your neck muscles take less strain overnight." | "Reduces inflammation" / "heals" | Treatment language. |
| "Comfortable support for a more curved neck." | "For scoliosis, sleep apnoea, post-surgery…" | Lists a medical condition as the target user. |
| "If pain lasts more than a few weeks or spreads to your arm, see a doctor." | "Try our pillow before seeing a doctor." | HSA: never discourage seeking medical advice. |
| "Research shows pillow height changes neck angle; it has not shown one pillow type is best." | "Clinically proven" / "doctor-recommended" / "orthopaedic pillow" | No trial of this pillow, and an implied endorsement. |
| "Checked for accuracy by <physio> (see About this guide)." | "Recommended by physiotherapists" | AHPC 5.7.2: no appearance of endorsement. |
