## Goal

The owner goes round the loop once on production and it holds together.

## Walk

1. Open the CRM group on desktop and on a phone. Every page opens; badges show.
2. Open a Result, then its Customer. Note their stage and next step.
3. Open a Return, then its Customer and its Order.
4. Choose *At risk*, save it as a Segment, *Email this Segment*, send yourself a test.
5. Read the Retention card; open *Bought once* from it.
6. As a Staff role without `campaigns`, confirm no *Email this* action appears.
