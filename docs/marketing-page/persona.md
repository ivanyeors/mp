# Marketing personas (canonical)

Source of truth: Strategic Growth roadmap + `CONTEXT.md` Marketing terms. Use these when writing or reviewing storefront copy.

## Shared pain pattern: Elusive Sleeper

Under all three personas: habitual pillow buyers who never find the right fit; chronic discomfort; need customisation (loft, firmness, contour) and often bolsters as part of a sleep system. Budget roughly $100–$600 when the product clearly solves the problem.

## 1. Affluent Wellness / Pain Sufferers (home hero lead)

- Chronic neck pain / stiffness; receptive to Premium bespoke and in-person fitting as a clinical signal.
- Lead the **first viewport** and Premium path (hero, Size Matters, credibility, clinician review, founder, appointment CTAs).
- Brand frame: measurement-led cervical fit, not gift copy. Never use "Science of Sleep" (retired).

## 2. Tech Neck professionals

- PMEBs with prolonged device use and cervical strain; care about overnight recovery and materials (e.g. FibreCool on Premium).
- Weave **only** into Neck Pains trigger + **Cervical Neck Support** learn-more slide-in (may add a section there). No new pages.

## 3. Multi-generational families

- Households / aging members / kids-height sizing; collective sleep support.
- Weave **only** into **Mbolster** surfaces. Do not invent Family Sleep Bundles in copy unless a live offer exists.

## Copy rules

1. Empathize with failed one-size purchases; lead with customisation and cervical alignment.
2. Stay claim-safe. Neck pain is the reader's problem, never the product's promise: say support, alignment, comfort, fit, less strain and sleep quality. Never relieve, treat, cure, heal, correct or rehab; never pain-free, orthopaedic pillow, clinically proven or doctor-recommended; never a medical condition as who a product is for (point people with one to their doctor). HSA classifies a product presented for pain relief as a medical device (GL-06). `lib/claims.ts` holds the banned list and `lib/claims.check.ts` enforces it on site copy.
3. Every public string should be SEO/SEM/AEO-friendly: answer-first ledes, natural Singapore high-intent keywords, extractable facts.
4. Consumer ambassadors ≠ Local Ambassadors (clinical partners on `/our-partners`).
