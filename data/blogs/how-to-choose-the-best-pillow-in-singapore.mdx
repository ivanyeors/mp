You wake with a stiff neck, or turn your pillow over at 3am looking for the cool side. The pillow may simply be wrong for you, rather than bad in general. Fit is personal: the same pillow can suit one person and leave their partner sore.

This guide takes the decision in the order that matters. First how you sleep, then height and firmness, then filling, then keeping cool and clean in Singapore's climate. It ends with when a custom pillow is worth paying for, and when it is not.

## What decides whether a pillow suits you?

Three things decide it: the height your head rests at, how well the pillow keeps that height overnight, and what it is filled with. Brand, price and the word "ergonomic" on the label matter far less.

The goal is the same for every sleeper. Your head should stay in line with your spine, neither tipped down towards the mattress nor pushed up. [[cite: a pillow should keep the neck in line with the chest and back | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852]] What changes from person to person is how much pillow it takes to get there.

Research has not settled on one ideal pillow height. A 2021 review of pillow-height studies found that body size, neck alignment, contact pressure and muscle activity all play a part. The evidence could not yet identify an optimal range. [[cite: Lei et al. 2021 Healthcare review: body dimension, cervical alignment, contact pressure and muscle activity are determinants of pillow height; an optimal range cannot yet be identified | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8544534/]] So a pillow that suits a friend tells you little about whether it will suit you.

## How does your sleep position change the pillow you need?

Your sleep position sets the height you need more than anything else. Side sleeping needs the most height, back sleeping a moderate amount, and stomach sleeping the least.

| Sleep position | What the pillow has to do | Height | Firmness | Common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Side | Fill the gap between your ear and the outside of your shoulder | Highest | Medium-firm to firm | A pillow that sinks, so the head drops towards the mattress |
| Back | Support the curve of the neck without pushing the chin forward | Medium | Medium | Stacking two pillows, which bends the neck forward |
| Stomach | Keep the head close to flat while it is turned to one side | Lowest, or none | Soft | Any pillow thick enough to tilt the head back |
| Side and back | Give two heights: higher at the sides, lower in the middle | Varies across the pillow | Medium | One even height that suits neither position |

### Side sleepers

When you lie on your side, your shoulder holds your upper body off the mattress. The pillow has to make up that distance, so broad shoulders need more height than narrow ones. Our guide to the [best pillow for side sleepers](/blog/side-sleeper-pillow-your-ultimate-guide-for-alignment-pain-relief) covers this in detail, including what to do with your knees.

### Back sleepers

On your back, your head needs less lift, but the hollow behind your neck still needs filling. A pillow that is slightly higher under the neck than under the head supports that curve. [[cite: back sleepers: rounded support under the neck curve with a flatter area under the head | https://www.health.harvard.edu/pain/say-good-night-to-neck-pain]] If your chin drifts towards your chest, the pillow is too high.

### Stomach sleepers

Face down, you have to turn your head fully to one side to breathe, and holding that twist for hours is hard on the neck. [[cite: stomach sleeping requires turning the head to one side, which strains the neck | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/sleeping-on-stomach]] A very thin pillow, or none, at least stops the head tilting back as well. Mpillow's own advice to stomach sleepers is to try moving to side sleeping.

### Combination sleepers

If you fall asleep on your back and wake on your side, one even height will be wrong for half the night. Look for a pillow that is higher at the edges than in the centre, or one you can adjust section by section.

## How high should your pillow be?

Your pillow should be high enough that, in your usual position, your nose lines up with the centre of your chest. Shoulder width, mattress softness and head shape all change how much pillow that takes.

A simple check works better than any chart. Lie in your normal position for a few minutes, then ask someone to photograph you from the foot of the bed.

- **On your side:** the line from your nose to your breastbone should run level with the mattress. If your head tips down, the pillow is too low. If it tips up, it is too high.
- **On your back:** your face should point at the ceiling, neither tucked towards your chest nor tilted back.
- **Either way:** sliding a hand under the pillow, or folding it in half at night, usually means it lacks height.

Your mattress changes the answer. A soft mattress lets your shoulder sink, which narrows the gap, so you need a little less pillow. A firm mattress keeps your shoulder up, so you need a little more. The Premium Mpillow configurator asks how firm your bed is, alongside your measurements.

At Mpillow, height starts from one measurement: the length across your shoulders. The Premium configurator adds three more, for neck length and for how far your neck and head sit from your upper back.

## How firm should a pillow be?

A pillow should be firm enough to keep its height under the weight of your head all night. How soft the surface feels is a separate question of comfort.

A pillow that starts at the right height but slowly sinks will be too low by morning. That is how a soft pillow most often lets a side sleeper down. Firmness and height also trade off: a tall, soft pillow and a lower, firmer one can hold your head at the same height.

Test a pillow for five minutes in your usual position, not five seconds. If your head keeps sinking, or you feel the urge to bunch the pillow up, it is too soft for you.

## What are the main types of pillow filling?

The fillings you will see most often in Singapore are memory foam, latex, polyester or microfibre, down and feather, buckwheat, and modular pouches. Each trades support, airflow and ease of washing differently.

| Filling | Feel | Holds its height | Airflow | Washing | Main drawback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Memory foam block | Slow, moulding, dense | Well | Low; can feel warm | Cover only; the foam core does not go in the machine | One fixed height you cannot change |
| Shredded memory foam | Mouldable, softer than a block | Fairly well | Better than a block | Check the label | Pieces shift and clump |
| Latex | Springy, pushes back | Well | Good, especially with pin-hole cores | Cover only; spot-clean the core | Heavy; unsuitable for latex allergy |
| Polyester or microfibre | Soft, light | Poorly over time | Moderate | Often machine washable | Flattens and lumps sooner |
| Down and feather | Very soft, squashable | Poorly; needs plumping | Moderate | Some are washable but slow to dry | Little structure for side sleepers |
| Buckwheat hulls | Firm, mouldable, rustles | Well | Good | Case only; hulls are aired or topped up | Heavy and noisy |
| Modular pouches | Depends on the mix | Well, and adjustable | Depends on the fibres | Components wash separately | Needs fitting to get right |

### What does research say about filling type?

Only a few trials have compared fillings, and they are small. In one Australian trial, side sleepers tried five pillow types for a week each. A latex pillow did best for waking symptoms, and a feather pillow did worst. [[cite: Gordon et al. 2010 J Pain Res field trial in side sleepers: latex performed best, feather least well for waking stiffness, headache and scapular/arm pain | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3004642/]]

A 2021 review concluded that pillow shape and height matter more for neck alignment than the material inside. [[cite: Pang et al. 2021 Clinical Biomechanics systematic review: side-lying cervical alignment impacted by pillow shape and height rather than rubber vs feather material | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33895703/]] So choose a filling for comfort and practicality, but get the height right first.

### What should you know about each filling?

- **Memory foam** moulds to your head and holds a steady height. Dense foam holds warmth, though, and a block's height cannot be changed if it is wrong for you.
- **Latex** is springy and keeps its shape. It is heavy, and people with a latex allergy can react to it, so check with your allergy specialist first. [[cite: people with latex allergy may react to latex pillows and mattresses | https://www.allergyuk.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Rubber-Latex-Allergy.pdf]]
- **Polyester and microfibre** are light and easy to wash, but loose fibre shifts and flattens. Fibre packed into separate compartments or pouches holds its shape better.
- **Down and feather** suit people who like to scrunch a soft pillow, but give little structure for side sleepers and need plumping.
- **Buckwheat** gives firm, mouldable support with air between the hulls, but is heavy and noisy. Buckwheat husk pillows have been linked to allergic reactions in people sensitised to buckwheat. [[cite: buckwheat husk pillows linked to asthma and sensitisation in people allergic to buckwheat | https://www.anaphylaxis.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Buckwheat-Allergy-Factsheet.pdf]]
- **Modular pouches** let height and feel change piece by piece. The Mpillow mixes softer hollow-fibre pouches and firmer fibre-ball pouches across three compartments. Our team confirms the mix for each pillow; you choose the cover and the case.

## How do you sleep cooler in Singapore's humidity?

Pick a pillow that lets air move, use a case that copes with sweat, and keep both clean. In Singapore the problem is humidity as much as heat, because sweat evaporates slowly in humid air. [[cite: humidity adds to thermal load during sleep by limiting sweat evaporation | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3427038/]]

A review of laboratory studies found that humid heat increases time awake and reduces deep and REM sleep. [[cite: Okamoto-Mizuno and Mizuno 2012 review: humid heat increases wakefulness and decreases slow-wave and REM sleep | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3427038/]] A pillow will not cool a room, but it can avoid adding to the problem.

- **Choose airflow over density.** A solid memory foam block holds warmth against your head. Latex with pin holes, buckwheat and fibre pouches leave more room for air.
- **Use a breathable case.** Cotton and linen breathe and wash easily. The Premium Mpillow comes with a linen case or a FibreCool case, which we describe as helpful if you run warm.
- **Wash the case often.** A spare case makes weekly washing easy.
- **Dry the filling right through.** Filling that stays damp after washing will start to smell, so wash on a dry, sunny day.
- **Keep sweat out of the filling.** The Mpillow's inner protector is waterproof, with filter holes to let air through.

Dust mites also prefer warm, humid homes, which is one more reason to choose a pillow whose parts you can wash. [[cite: house dust mites prefer warm, humid conditions; washing bedding and barrier covers reduce allergen | https://www.allergyuk.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/House-Dust-Mites.pdf]] If you have a dust mite allergy, ask your doctor whether allergen-proof covers would help.

## How long should a pillow last?

A pillow is due for replacement when it no longer holds the height you need, whatever its age. Fillings wear at different rates, and with a modular pillow, worn parts can be replaced instead of the whole pillow.

## What pillow should a child use?

Babies under 12 months should not sleep with a pillow at all. [[cite: AAP safe sleep: infants up to 1 year should sleep without pillows or soft bedding | https://healthychildren.org/English/news/Pages/safe-sleep-back-is-best-avoid-soft-bedding-inclined-surfaces-and-bedsharing.aspx]] Older children have narrower shoulders than adults, so they need a lower pillow, and the right height changes as they grow. ## When does a custom pillow make sense?

A custom pillow makes sense when shop-bought heights miss your body, or when you sleep in two positions and need two heights. If a regular pillow already lets you wake comfortable, you do not need one.

A custom pillow is most likely to help if:

- you have tried several pillows and none keep your head level;
- your shoulders are noticeably broader or narrower than average;
- you switch between side and back during the night;
- your pillows flatten within months and you keep replacing them.

## What if you already have neck pain?

The right pillow can reduce strain on your neck at night, but it does not remove the cause of neck pain. See a doctor or physiotherapist if pain lasts more than a few weeks or keeps coming back. Do the same after an injury, or if you have numbness, tingling or weakness in your arms. [[cite: NHS neck pain: see a GP if pain does not improve after a few weeks, or with pins and needles, numbness or weakness | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/neck-pain/]]

## Frequently asked questions

### What pillow height do side sleepers need?

Side sleepers need a pillow that fills the gap between ear and outer shoulder, so the head stays level with the spine. Broader shoulders and firmer mattresses call for more height.

### Is memory foam a good choice if I sleep hot?

A solid memory foam block is dense and lets little air through, so it tends to hold warmth. If you run warm, shredded foam, latex with pin holes, buckwheat or fibre pouches are usually cooler choices.

### Which pillow filling is easiest to keep clean?

Fibre fillings and modular pouches are usually easiest, because the filling itself can be washed. Memory foam and latex cores cannot go in the washing machine, so you rely on washing the cover.

### Can one pillow suit both side and back sleeping?

Yes, if it has more than one height. A pillow that is higher at the sides than in the centre lets you roll between positions without swapping pillows. The Mpillow's three-zone M shape is built this way.

### Should a stomach sleeper use a pillow?

A stomach sleeper needs a very thin, soft pillow or none, so the head is not tilted back as well as turned. Mpillow recommends trying to move to side sleeping.

### What is the best pillow for neck pain?

The best pillow for neck pain is one that keeps your neck level in your usual sleep position. A pillow can reduce strain, but persistent pain, numbness or tingling needs a doctor or physiotherapist.
