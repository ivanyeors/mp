If you suffer from chronic neck pain, you may already know the importance of using a cervical neck support pillow. However, many people overlook the benefits of pairing it with a bolster for added comfort and alignment.
A bolster is a cylindrical pillow that can be used in various ways to enhance spinal support. For side sleepers, placing a bolster between the knees can help keep the spine properly aligned, reducing strain on the neck and back. This positioning helps prevent pressure points and promotes better circulation, which contributes to overall neck pain relief.
For back sleepers, a bolster can be placed under the knees to reduce tension in the lower back, which indirectly helps improve neck posture. By keeping the spine aligned from top to bottom, a bolster minimizes the risk of waking up with stiffness or discomfort. Additionally, for individuals who like to lean on something while sleeping, a bolster provides the perfect amount of support for the shoulders and arms, further reducing pressure on the neck.
The best part about using a bolster is that it can be customized to your preferred firmness, ensuring that you get the right amount of support. Unlike standard pillows that may lose shape over time, an adjustable bolster allows you to maintain proper spinal alignment based on your body’s needs.
If you’re struggling with neck pain relief, consider adding a bolster to your sleep setup. Whether you are a back or side sleeper, this simple addition can make a significant difference in improving sleep posture, reducing stiffness, and helping you wake up feeling refreshed.
