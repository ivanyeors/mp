No two people lie on a bed in quite the same way. A pillow that suits one sleeper can leave another with a stiff neck. The Mpillow deals with this through configurations, matched to your size and body profile.

This guide is for anyone new to the Mpillow. It explains how your configuration is chosen, what to expect when you are measured, and how to find your size again. For the wider picture of how a fitted pillow is made, see [how custom-made pillows are crafted](/blog/tailored-to-you-understanding-the-process-of-crafting-custom-made-pillows-for-better-sleep).

## What is an Mpillow configuration?

An Mpillow configuration is the way the filling packs are arranged inside your pillow. Mpillow has developed more than 50 configurations, for round or flat heads, curved or straight spines, long or short necks, and thick or flat backs.

Every Mpillow has three compartments that hold the fillings and keep the pillow's shape. The fillings are softer hollow-fibre packs and firmer fibre-ball packs, mixed to set the height and feel.

You do not pick the fillings yourself. Mpillow staff confirm the filling mix for your configuration, and you choose the cover and the case.

## Why does your Mpillow need to be configured?

Your Mpillow needs to be configured because the points where your body presses into the bed differ from person to person. Configuring the pillow places support around those pressure points.

Mpillow recommends two sleep positions, on your side and on your back, and the pillow's zones are built for both:

- **On your back**, the centre zone supports the curve of your neck.
- **On your side**, the left and right zones sit at a height set by your shoulder length, to help keep your neck in line.

If you usually sleep on your stomach, Mpillow suggests trying to sleep on your side instead. Back sleepers may also need support for the lower back.

## How does Mpillow work out your configuration?

Mpillow works out your configuration in three stages, all from your measurements:

1. **Your pillow size**, set by your shoulder length.
2. **Your body profile type**, read from your measurements.
3. **Your configuration type**, based on your size and your profile type.

How many measurements this takes depends on the tier:

| Tier | Measurements taken | Where they are taken | Cervical Neck Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Mpillow | Shoulder length only | Online, in the Basic configurator | Not available |
| Premium Mpillow | Shoulder length, neck to back, head to back and neck length | Online in the Premium configurator, or by our team at a studio measurement | Optional, placed inside by staff, for a more curved neck |

The Cervical Neck Support is a Premium accessory that also helps stop the fillings flattening. Our guide to [cervical support pillows](/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-cervical-support-pillows-for-neck-pain-relief) explains how this kind of support works.

After you submit your measurements online, Mpillow places an authorisation hold. Staff then assign your size and filling mix, and you review the final build before payment is taken. If you are unsure of a measurement, you can use your best estimate and refine the fit later at a re-adjustment.

## What happens at a customisation appointment?

At a customisation appointment, Mpillow staff take your measurements and build your filling mix while you watch. You then try the Mpillow until you are happy with the fit. By the end you should know:

- your Mpillow profile type;
- the pillow size you sleep on;
- how your Mpillow is configured, and how it can be adjusted;
- how to wash and care for it.

A few practical points help the appointment go smoothly:

- **Booking:** choose a location, date and time on the [customisation appointment page](/appointments?type=customisation). You get a calendar invite and WhatsApp reminders.
- **What to wear:** something comfortable, since you will try the pillow after you are measured.
- **Two people:** one slot covers one person, so book two adjacent slots.
- **Rescheduling:** message Mpillow on WhatsApp at least 48 hours ahead.
- **Measurement service:** a fee applies only when our team measures you. It is paid with your pillow, not on the day, and measuring yourself online is free.
- **Outside Singapore:** submit your measurements online; bookings from overseas are done by video call.

## How do I find my Mpillow size again?

Your order records the pillow you bought. Sign in to your Mpillow account to see it, or check your invoice. If you bought in store, before accounts existed or as a gift, keep your receipt as your record.

To check your size against your shoulder length, use the chart from the Basic Mpillow page:

| Size | Shoulder length |
| --- | --- |
| XS | Under 14" |
| S | 15" to 16" |
| M | 17" to 18" |
| L | 19" to 20" |
| XL | 21" to 22" |

If you fall between two sizes, round up: a 16.4" shoulder length takes size M. If the chart and the configurator ever differ, follow the configurator, because Mpillow staff maintain the sizing rules behind it.

## How do I adjust the side compartments of my Mpillow?

The simplest way is to book a re-adjustment, where Mpillow staff re-tune the compartments for you. For the Premium Mpillow, extra stuffing is free during a re-adjustment, though accessories are not included.

The Premium Mpillow includes one complimentary re-adjustment within 60 days of purchase. After that, and for the Basic Mpillow and the Mbolster, a re-adjustment fee applies.

The three compartments are designed to make the height easy to adjust. The right fill depends on your size and profile type, such as a long or a short neck, so no single chart suits everyone. For washing, the [Mpillow aftercare video](/mp-care) shows how to clean the pillow.

## How is the Mbolster sized?

The Mbolster is sized by your height, not your shoulders, and uses the same modular fillings as the Mpillow:

- **S:** children, or anyone 1.50 m and below.
- **M:** 1.50 m to 1.80 m.
- **L:** above 1.80 m.

The Mbolster's firmness is tuned for side sleeping at a re-adjustment, which you book the same way as one for the Mpillow.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Mpillow configurations are there?

Mpillow has developed more than 50 configurations. They cover round or flat heads, curved or straight spines, long or short necks, and thick or flat backs.

### Can two people be measured in one appointment?

No. One appointment slot covers one person, so two people should book two slots next to each other.

### What if my shoulder length falls between two Mpillow sizes?

Round up to the larger size. For example, a shoulder length of 16.4 inches takes size M.

### Can my Mpillow configuration be changed later?

Yes. Book a re-adjustment and Mpillow staff will re-tune your pillow. For the Premium Mpillow, extra stuffing is free during a re-adjustment, though accessories are not included.
