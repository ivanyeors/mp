If you’re still using a traditional body pillow or bolster, it might be time to consider upgrading to something more supportive, hygienic, and tailored to your sleep needs — like an adjustable bolster.
Unlike conventional bolsters that come with a fixed size and firmness, adjustable bolsters such as the Mbolster are designed with customisability in mind. Whether you prefer a firmer feel to support your knees while side sleeping, or a softer one to hug during rest, a customisable bolster gives you control. You can easily add or remove filling to suit your comfort level — ensuring you get the exact firmness that works for your body and sleeping posture.
Another major advantage of an adjustable bolster is hygiene. Most traditional bolsters are difficult to clean thoroughly — often leading to a build-up of sweat, bacteria, or allergens over time. The Mbolster, however, is designed to be machine washable inside and out, making it a much more hygienic option. This is especially helpful for those with allergies or sensitive skin.
What makes Mbolster even more versatile is that it comes in three sizes — S, M, and L — to fit sleepers of different heights. Whether you’re a petite sleeper or someone taller who needs more coverage and support, there’s a perfect fit for you.
If better sleep, cleaner bedding, and personalised comfort are your priorities, then an adjustable bolster is a game-changer. It’s more than just a pillow — it’s a tailored sleep solution that truly supports your rest, recovery, and overall well-being.
