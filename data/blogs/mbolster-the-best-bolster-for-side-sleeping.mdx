What is a bolster for side sleeping?


A bolster for side sleeping is a long, supportive pillow designed to keep your body aligned while sleeping on your side.
It provides comfort to your shoulders, spine, and hips by filling the gap between your arms and torso.
Hugging a bolster reduces shoulder pressure and prevents your body from twisting during sleep.


Why should side sleepers use a bolster?


Helps maintain proper spinal alignment throughout the night.
Reduces neck and shoulder tension by preventing your upper shoulder from collapsing.
Provides comfort and body stability, leading to deeper and more restful sleep.
Encourages a natural sleeping posture that reduces morning stiffness and soreness.
