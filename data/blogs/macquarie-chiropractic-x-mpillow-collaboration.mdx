Long days at a desk can leave your neck and shoulders stiff by evening. Macquarie Chiropractic, a clinic on Robinson Road, set out to serve exactly those desk-bound professionals in Singapore. If that sounds like you, our guide to [tech neck from desk and phone use](/blog/what-is-tech-neck-and-how-can-a-pillow-help-fix-it-in-singapore) explains what that posture does to the neck.

In 2019 the clinic celebrated its first anniversary. We at Mellow Life, the company behind Mpillow, were glad to be part of the event. This post is our account of that collaboration.

## Who is Macquarie Chiropractic?

Macquarie Chiropractic is a chiropractic clinic on Robinson Road in Singapore. When we worked with it in 2019, it focused on chiropractic care for desk-bound professionals.

We found the clinic's chiropractors highly trained and deeply committed to their work. The atmosphere was positive and lively, which we put down to a dedicated team and its care for patients.

We were also impressed by the clinic's modern equipment, its careful hygiene and the experience of its chiropractors. The team enjoyed serving the community, and regularly shared posture insights and tips on social media.

## What happened at the anniversary event?

Macquarie Chiropractic marked its first year with an event at the clinic in 2019 and invited Mpillow to take part. The event gave us a platform to show the Mpillow to the clinic's chiropractors.

In 2019, patients could also buy the Mpillow at the clinic. We are grateful to the Macquarie team for their support, and for letting us share in their celebration.

## What did the chiropractors think of the Mpillow?

The chiropractors tried the Mpillow at the event and gave us positive feedback. What follows is Mpillow's summary of their views, not their own words:

- They were impressed by the pillow's design and quality.
- They were convinced its shape and flexibility could help people get a good night's sleep.
- They felt it could help people avoid waking with body aches and stiffness, and even support better posture.

These were impressions from trying the pillow on the day, not findings from a study. A pillow is only one part of good posture, and our guide to [good posture habits and simple exercises](/blog/improve-good-posture-habits-with-exercises-and-home-remedies) covers the daytime side. If neck or back pain persists, or comes with numbness, tingling or weakness, see a doctor or physiotherapist.
