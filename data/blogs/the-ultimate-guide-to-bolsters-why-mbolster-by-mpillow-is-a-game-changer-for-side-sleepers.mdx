Why Bolsters Are Essential for Side Sleepers
For side sleepers, having the right support is essential. When you sleep on your side, there is a natural gap between your legs and a tendency for your spine to fall out of alignment. A bolster can help bridge this gap, supporting the legs and ensuring proper spinal alignment. This added support helps relieve pressure on the lower back and hips, reducing discomfort and strain on the body. Additionally, hugging a bolster can have a calming, comforting effect, which can contribute to better sleep quality.
But not all bolsters are created equal. Many traditional bolsters come with a fixed filling and firmness, making them difficult to customize to individual preferences. This is where the Mbolster by Mpillow shines—thanks to its adjustable firmness and removable filling, the Mbolster is a versatile, supportive, and hygienic choice for side sleepers who want a personalized sleep solution.
