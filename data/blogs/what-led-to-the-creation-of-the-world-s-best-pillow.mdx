If you have ever lain awake with a sore neck, swapping one pillow for another, the start of the Mpillow will sound familiar. "Necessity is the mother of invention," as the proverb goes, and it sums up how the Mpillow came about.

This page tells that story, from one engineer's sleepless nights to the modular pillow Mpillow offers today.

## Who founded Mpillow, and why?

Darren Yeo founded Mpillow because of his own chronic neck pain. He is an engineer in the precision cutting tools industry, and he started work on the pillow in 2016.

Before that, Darren had many sleepless nights, and they took a toll on his health and his productivity during the day. The conventional pillows he tried did not help. He still woke with severe neck pain and an aching body.

He soon found he was not alone. Many people dreaded going to bed because of the aches it brought, or simply could not sleep well. That pushed him to design a pillow that would give people the restful sleep they wanted.

## What did Darren learn before designing the Mpillow?

Darren learned that every body is shaped differently. He began by taking courses in ergonomics and studying different body structures and sleep postures.

That study showed him that each person needs a different neck support and pillow height to hold a good posture in bed. The same idea sits behind every [Mpillow size and configuration](/blog/which-mpillow-configuration-is-for-you) today.

He also came to see hygiene as part of the problem. A conventional pillow is hard to keep clean once dust and dirt work into the filling. So he looked for ways to protect the filling and make every part washable.

## How was the Mpillow designed and tested?

Darren used his engineering background to build a pillow that brought these ideas together. He made a series of prototypes, put the designs through repeated tests, and asked chiropractors for feedback.

It was demanding work. Darren was developing the product while holding down his office job and looking after his family.

After years of research and design, the work paid off. He arrived at a design that gave him the sleep he needed, so he woke feeling rested rather than tired. That design rests on five ideas:

- **An M shape** that supports back and side sleeping in one pillow. The design is patented.
- **Three zones inside:** a centre zone for back sleeping, and left and right zones at different heights for side sleeping.
- **Modular filling packs** in three compartments, which hold the pillow's shape and make its height easy to adjust.
- **Five sizes**, matched to shoulder length.
- **Washable parts**, so the whole pillow can be taken apart and cleaned.

## What is the Mpillow today?

Today the Mpillow comes in two tiers, Basic and Premium, alongside the Mbolster, a bolster built on the same modular structure.

| Product | Sized from | What it offers |

| Basic Mpillow | Your shoulder length | An entry custom-fit pillow for occasional neck tension |
| Premium Mpillow | Your shoulder, neck and head measurements | Firmness set to your preference, plus an optional Cervical Neck Support for a more curved neck |
| Mbolster | Your height | A side-sleep bolster for children through adults, with adjustable firmness |

You choose the cover and the case. Mpillow staff confirm the filling mix from your measurements, so the inside is set for your body. You can measure at home with the guided configurator, or book a studio measurement.

The Mpillow received the Singapore Good Design Award 2022, and Mpillow works with chiropractors and physiotherapists in Singapore. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who makes the Mpillow?

Mpillow is run by Mellow Life Pte Ltd, a Singapore company. The pillow was created by Mpillow's founder, Darren Yeo, an engineer in the precision cutting tools industry.

### When was the Mpillow created?

Darren Yeo began work on the Mpillow in 2016, after years of struggling with neck pain and poor sleep. The finished design came out of years of research, prototypes and testing.

### Is the Mpillow design patented?

Yes. The Mpillow's modular M design is patented, and Mpillow describes it as patented across markets.

### Has the Mpillow won any awards?

Yes. The Mpillow received the Singapore Good Design Award 2022.
