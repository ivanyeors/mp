## How often should you replace your pillow?

Most pillows need replacing every one to two years, because the filling compresses and stops supporting your head the way it did when new. [[cite: most pillows need replacing within a year or two because the filling gives out and support drops; fold test (stays folded or goes flat = replace); lumpy or dented pillows are a warning sign | https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2026/04/08/signs-you-may-need-a-new-pillow]]

The filling sets how fast that happens. Treat the ranges below as a guide, not a deadline. A pillow that fails the checks in the next section is due, whatever its age.

| Filling | How it usually wears | Typical lifespan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polyester or fibrefill | Flattens and clumps | 6 months to 2 years |
| Feather or down | Loses loft and needs plumping | 1 to 3 years |
| Memory foam | Softens and keeps a dent | 2 to 3 years |
| Latex | Holds its shape longest, then loses spring | 2 to 4 years |
| Modular fibre pouches (Mpillow) | Individual pouches compress | No set interval: pouches are topped up, not the whole pillow |

The lifespans in the table are typical ranges for each filling, not tested limits. [[cite: typical pillow lifespans by filling: polyester 6 months to 2 years, feather/down 1 to 3 years, memory foam 2 to 3 years, latex 2 to 4 years, and how each wears | https://www.sleepfoundation.org/pillows/how-often-should-you-replace-your-pillow]]

## What are the signs your pillow needs replacing?

Replace a pillow when it no longer springs back, holds lumps or dips, or leaves you stiff in the morning. Any one of these is enough.

- **It fails the fold test.** Fold a fibre or feather pillow in half. If it stays folded or lies flat, the filling is spent, according to the Cleveland Clinic guidance above. Foam and latex do not fold, so judge them by dents and lost bounce.
- **It has lumps, dips or a permanent dent.** Your head sinks into the low spot and your neck bends to follow it.
- **You stack or fold it to get enough height.** The filling has compressed below the height you need.
- **It smells musty or stays stained after washing.** Sweat and moisture have reached the filling, not just the case.
- **You wake stiff, and it eases as the day goes on.** That pattern is worth checking against your pillow first.
- **You sneeze or feel stuffy in bed.** Dust mite allergy causes hay-fever-like symptoms such as sneezing, a runny nose and itchy eyes. The mites live mainly in bedding, soft furniture and carpet. [[cite: dust mite allergy symptoms (sneezing, runny nose, itchy or watery eyes); mites eat shed skin cells, like warm humid places, and live mostly in bedding, padded furniture and carpeting | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/dust-mites/symptoms-causes/syc-20352173]]

A bolster wears in the same way, so check it for lumps, flat spots and smells too.

## Why does a worn-out pillow matter for neck pain?

A flattened pillow lets your head drop out of line with your spine, so your neck spends the night bent. A Cleveland Clinic chiropractor names a pillow that bends your neck forward or to one side as the most common pillow mistake. [[cite: most common mistake is a pillow that bends the neck forward or to one side; too low points the jaw to the ceiling, too high points it to the chest | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/is-your-pillow-hurting-your-neck-7-tips-for-better-sleep]]

For back sleepers, the chin is a quick check. Tipped towards the ceiling suggests the pillow is too low; tucked towards the chest suggests too high.

Side sleepers need more height, because the pillow has to fill the gap between the shoulder and the head. That is why the Mpillow's height is set from your shoulder length. Our page on [pillow height for side sleepers](/blog/side-sleeper-pillow-your-ultimate-guide-for-alignment-pain-relief) goes into more detail.

A new pillow can reduce strain, but it will not settle every stiff neck. See a doctor or physiotherapist if neck pain lasts more than a few weeks or follows an injury. Go sooner if it comes with numbness, tingling or arm weakness. [[cite: see a GP if neck pain does not improve after a few weeks; get urgent advice for numbness, tingling or weakness in the arms, or pain after an injury or fall | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/neck-pain/]] Our guide to [the best pillow for neck pain](/blog/what-is-the-best-pillow-for-neck-pain-in-singapore) covers height and firmness in depth.

## How often should you wash your pillow and bedding?

Wash pillowcases and sheets every week, and the pillow itself whenever its care label allows. Mayo Clinic advises washing bedding weekly in water of at least 54.4°C (130°F) to kill dust mites and remove allergens. [[cite: wash sheets, blankets, pillowcases and bedcovers weekly in water of at least 130°F (54.4°C); use allergen-proof covers on pillows and mattresses; keep indoor humidity below 50% (a dehumidifier or air conditioner helps); vacuum regularly; mites cannot be eliminated but can be greatly reduced | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/dust-mites/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20352178]]

Pillows can hold more than mites. A study of ten used feather and synthetic pillows grew between 4 and 16 species of fungi from each one. [[cite: Woodcock et al., Allergy 2006: 10 used pillows (1.5 to over 20 years old) cultured; 4 to 16 fungal species per pillow, more in synthetic pillows | https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/fungal-contamination-of-bedding/]]

So dry every part completely before you put the pillow back together. In a humid bedroom, filling that goes back damp stays damp.

| What | How often | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pillowcases and sheets | Weekly | Hot wash if the fabric allows |
| Pillow protector | As its care label says | A waterproof one keeps sweat and saliva off the filling |
| Pillow insert | As its care label says | Dry completely before re-casing |

## Why do dust mites matter more in Singapore?

House dust mites are the main allergy trigger found in Singapore studies, and they thrive in warm, humid homes. In a study of more than 7,000 people in Singapore, allergic sensitisation was directed almost entirely at house dust mites. [[cite: Andiappan et al., Allergy 2014: two Singapore Chinese cohorts (576 and 7,373 people); IgE sensitisation almost exclusively against house dust mite allergens; over 80% HDM-sIgE positive | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24456108]]

You cannot remove every mite, but the Mayo Clinic guidance cited above shows how to cut their numbers:

- Wash bedding weekly in hot water.
- Use allergen-proof covers on pillows and mattresses.
- Keep indoor humidity below 50%. In Singapore, air-conditioning or a dehumidifier helps.
- Vacuum the mattress and bedroom regularly.

If sneezing or a blocked nose in bed keeps coming back, mention it to your doctor.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it bad to sleep on an old pillow?

An old pillow is not dangerous in itself, but a flattened one can let your neck bend all night. Replace it if it fails the fold test, has lumps, or leaves you stiff.

### How long does an Mpillow last?

Mpillow does not publish a fixed lifespan, because worn filling pouches are topped up at a re-adjustment rather than the pillow being replaced. The Premium Mpillow has a 10-year warranty on filling height, and re-adjustment fees still apply.

### How often should I wash my pillowcase?

Wash it weekly, in hot water if the fabric allows. Weekly hot washing is one of the main ways to keep dust mites down where you sleep.

### Does a pillow protector help with dust mites?

An allergen-proof cover helps, and Mayo Clinic recommends one for pillows and mattresses alongside weekly hot washing. If mites are your concern, check that a protector is labelled allergen-proof, not just waterproof.

### Should I replace my bolster as often as my pillow?

Check a bolster the same way: lumps, flat spots and smells. The Mbolster's fillings come out, so the bolster can go in the washing machine and its firmness can be adjusted.
