You wake with a stiff neck and catch yourself folding the pillow in half to get enough height. Or you notice the pillow has gone yellow, and you cannot remember when you bought it.

This page covers how long pillows last, how each filling wears and the signs one is worn out. It also covers washing, dust mites, and what changes with a modular pillow. It is part of our guide on [how to choose a pillow in Singapore](/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-pillow-in-singapore).

## How long do pillows last?

Most pillows are due within a year or two ([Cleveland Clinic](https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2026/04/08/signs-you-may-need-a-new-pillow)). Over that time the filling compresses and stops holding your head where it did when new.

Each filling wears in its own way, as the table below shows. [Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/pain/say-good-night-to-neck-pain) notes that feather pillows collapse over time and should be replaced every year or so. For other fillings, judge by condition rather than age: a pillow that fails the checks in the next section is due, whatever its age.

| Filling | How it usually wears | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polyester or fibrefill | Flattens and clumps | Fold test, lumps |
| Feather or down | Loses loft and needs plumping | Fold test, flat spots |
| Memory foam | Softens and keeps a dent | Dents, slow or no spring back |
| Latex | Loses its spring | Dents, lost bounce |
| Modular fibre pouches (Mpillow) | Individual pouches compress | Height; worn pouches are topped up, not the whole pillow |

## What are the signs your pillow needs replacing?

Replace a pillow when it no longer springs back, holds lumps or dips, or leaves you stiff in the morning. Any one of these is enough.

- **It fails the fold test.** Fold a fibre or feather pillow in half. If it stays folded or lies flat, the filling is spent, according to the Cleveland Clinic guidance above. Foam and latex do not fold, so judge them by dents and lost bounce.
- **It has lumps, dips or a permanent dent.** Your head sinks into the low spot and your neck bends to follow it.
- **You stack or fold it to get enough height.** The filling has compressed below the height you need.
- **It smells musty or stays stained after washing.** Sweat and moisture have reached the filling, not just the case.
- **You wake stiff, and it eases as the day goes on.** That pattern is worth checking against your pillow first.
- **You sneeze or feel stuffy in bed.** The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/allergic-rhinitis/) lists house dust mites among the common causes of allergic rhinitis: sneezing, an itchy, runny or blocked nose, and itchy, red, watery eyes.

A bolster wears in the same way, so check it for lumps, flat spots and smells too.

## Why does a worn-out pillow matter for neck pain?

A flattened pillow lets your head drop out of line with your spine, so your neck spends the night bent. A [Cleveland Clinic chiropractor](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/is-your-pillow-hurting-your-neck-7-tips-for-better-sleep) names a pillow that bends your neck forward or to one side as the most common pillow mistake.

For back sleepers, the chin is a quick check. Tipped towards the ceiling suggests the pillow is too low; tucked towards the chest suggests too high.

Side sleepers need more height, because the pillow has to fill the gap between the shoulder and the head. That is why the Mpillow's height is set from your shoulder length. Our page on [pillow height for side sleepers](/blog/side-sleeper-pillow-your-ultimate-guide-for-alignment-pain-relief) goes into more detail.

A new pillow can reduce strain, but it will not settle every stiff neck. See a doctor or physiotherapist if neck pain lasts more than a few weeks or follows an injury. Go sooner if it comes with numbness, tingling or arm weakness. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/neck-pain-and-stiff-neck/) advises a GP visit for neck pain lasting beyond a few weeks. Its [whiplash guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/whiplash/) asks for urgent help if your arms feel weak or tingle after a neck injury. ## How often should you wash your pillow and bedding?

Wash pillowcases and sheets every week, and the pillow itself whenever its care label allows. [Allergy UK](https://www.allergyuk.org/resources/house-dust-mite-allergy-factsheet/) notes that washing at 60°C or above kills dust mites. Cooler washes remove some allergen, but the mites survive.

Pillows can hold more than mites. A [2006 study](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/fungal-contamination-of-bedding/) of ten used feather and synthetic pillows grew between 4 and 16 species of fungi from each one.

So dry every part completely before you put the pillow back together. In a humid bedroom, filling that goes back damp stays damp.

| What | How often | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pillowcases and sheets | Weekly | Hot wash if the fabric allows |
| Pillow protector | As its care label says | A waterproof one keeps sweat and saliva off the filling |

## Why do dust mites matter more in Singapore?

Allergy UK notes that dust mites prefer warm, humid conditions, which describes most Singapore homes. In [a 2014 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24456108/) of two groups of ethnic Chinese people living in Singapore, more than 7,000 in all, allergic sensitisation was almost entirely to house dust mites.

You cannot remove every mite, but you can cut their numbers:

- Wash bedding at 60°C or above where the fabric allows, which Allergy UK says kills mites.
- Use breathable covers that fully enclose the pillow and mattress. Allergy UK advises choosing covers tested to keep mite allergen in.
- Keep indoor humidity down. In Singapore, air-conditioning or a dehumidifier helps.
- Vacuum the mattress and bedroom regularly.

If sneezing or a blocked nose in bed keeps coming back, mention it to your doctor.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it bad to sleep on an old pillow?

An old pillow is not dangerous in itself, but a flattened one can let your neck bend all night. Replace it if it fails the fold test, has lumps, or leaves you stiff.

### How long does an Mpillow last?

Mpillow does not publish a fixed lifespan, because worn filling pouches are topped up at a re-adjustment rather than the pillow being replaced. The Premium Mpillow has a 10-year warranty on filling height, and re-adjustment fees still apply.

### How often should I wash my pillowcase?

Wash it weekly, in hot water if the fabric allows. Weekly hot washing is one of the main ways to keep dust mites down where you sleep.

### Does a pillow protector help with dust mites?

It can, if it is the right kind. Allergy UK recommends breathable covers that fully enclose the pillow and are tested to keep mite allergen in. A protector that is only waterproof may not do this.

### Should I replace my bolster as often as my pillow?

Check a bolster the same way: lumps, flat spots and smells. The Mbolster's fillings come out, so the bolster can go in the washing machine and its firmness can be adjusted.
