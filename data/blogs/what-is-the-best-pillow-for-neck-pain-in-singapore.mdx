Waking up with a neck that will not turn, or a shoulder that aches before the alarm, makes for a long day. It is natural to blame the pillow, and sometimes the pillow really is part of the problem.

This guide explains what a pillow can and cannot do for neck and shoulder pain. It covers height, firmness, shape and fillings. It also covers how to tell when the pillow is not the cause, and when to see a doctor.

## Can the wrong pillow cause neck pain?

Yes, it can contribute. A pillow that is too high, too flat or worn out can hold your neck bent for hours. That often shows up as morning stiffness.

[Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/symptoms/neck-pain/basics/causes/sym-20050882) lists sleeping in an awkward position, or with too many or too few pillows, among the causes of neck pain.

Research on pillows is encouraging but mixed. A [2021 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33895703/) found that pillows reduced waking neck pain and neck disability in people with chronic neck pain. They did not change overall sleep quality. A [2025 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40633255/) found only small, non-significant changes in pain and called for better trials.

Sleep posture seems to matter too. In a [small study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8631621/) that filmed people asleep, those with neck symptoms spent more of the night in positions thought to strain the neck ([2024 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11218933/)).

A pillow is still only one factor. It cannot undo a day hunched over a laptop, an old injury or wear in the neck joints.

## What should a pillow do for your neck?

A good pillow keeps your head, neck and upper back in roughly the line they hold when you stand tall. Your neck muscles can then rest instead of holding your head in place all night.

[Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/neck-pain/symptoms-causes/syc-20375581) advises keeping your head and neck aligned with your body, with a small pillow under the neck.

For the neck, that means the pillow fills the space under your neck as well as under your head. A pillow that only props up the back of your head leaves the neck unsupported in between.

## How high should a pillow be for your sleep position?

Height is the first thing to get right for your neck. The right height depends on your sleep position and your build, so no single number suits everyone.

### Side sleepers: let your shoulders set the height

Side sleeping needs the most height, because your shoulder lifts your head off the mattress. Broad shoulders leave a bigger gap to fill than narrow ones.

[Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/pain/say-good-night-to-neck-pain) advises side sleepers to use a pillow that is higher under the neck than under the head.

Mpillow sets pillow height from your shoulder length for the same reason. Our [side sleeper pillow guide](/blog/side-sleeper-pillow-your-ultimate-guide-for-alignment-pain-relief) covers knee support and combination sleeping in more detail.

### Back sleepers: the risk is too much height

On your back, your neck needs less lift than on your side. The common mistake for the neck is too much height, which bends your chin towards your chest. Harvard Health warns that too high or stiff a pillow keeps the neck bent overnight, which can cause morning pain and stiffness.

### Stomach sleepers: the hardest position for the neck

Sleeping on your front keeps your head turned to one side for hours, and no pillow fully solves that. The neck pain advice from [HealthHub Singapore](https://www.healthhub.sg/health-conditions/neck-pain) is to try not to sleep on your stomach.

Mpillow recommends side or back sleeping, and suggests stomach sleepers try moving to their side. A bolster can make the change easier, as our guide to [using a bolster for side sleeping](/blog/how-to-use-a-bolster-pillow-for-better-side-sleeping-in-singapore-homes) shows.

## Should a pillow for neck pain be firm or soft?

Firm enough to hold your neck at the same height all night, and soft enough to be comfortable. For your neck, firmness matters mostly through what it does to height.

A pillow that sinks under your head lets your neck bend towards the mattress by morning. One that is too full or too stiff keeps your neck bent the other way.

Firmness is also personal, which is why adjustable filling helps. The Basic Mpillow is the softer of the two tiers. Premium firmness is customised with softer hollow-fibre pouches and firmer fibre-ball pouches.

## Is a contour or cervical pillow better than a regular one?

Not automatically. A contour pillow can suit back and side sleepers who like a defined neck roll. The shape alone has not been shown to beat a well-fitted regular pillow.

In a [2010 trial of side sleepers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3004642/), a foam contour pillow did no better than a regular foam pillow for waking symptoms. Height and fit matter more than the shape on the label.

## Does the pillow filling matter for neck pain?

Less than height and shape. A 2021 review of pillow studies suggested that a pillow's shape and height may affect neck alignment more than its material does.

For a sore neck, choose a filling that holds its height through the night and can be adjusted if the height is wrong. Every filling settles with use, so the right height on day one will not last forever.

## How can you stop shoulder pain when you sleep?

Start by keeping off the sore shoulder, then make sure your pillow is high enough for side sleeping.

In a [Danish study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22608285/) of 83 people with pain in one shoulder, two in three of those who slept on one side lay on the painful shoulder. The study could not show which came first.

Pillow choice may play a part too. In the 2010 trial of side sleepers, the type of pillow made a difference to shoulder-blade and arm pain on waking.

Things to try tonight:

- **Sleep on the other side or your back.** Ireland's public health service, the [HSE](https://www2.hse.ie/conditions/shoulder-pain/), advises not sleeping on the same side as your shoulder pain.
- **Stop yourself rolling over.** A pillow placed along your back makes it harder to roll onto the sore side ([University Hospitals Coventry and Warwickshire NHS Trust](https://www.uhcw.nhs.uk/self-care/shoulder/)).
- **Check the pillow height.** If your head drops towards the mattress on your side, the pillow is too low for your shoulders.
- **Use the bolster you already have.** Many Singapore homes sleep with a bolster. Mpillow supports the neck and the Mbolster supports the torso for side sleeping; see our [bolster buying guide](/blog/the-complete-guide-to-choosing-the-right-bolster-in-singapore).

The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shoulder-pain/) advises seeing a GP if shoulder pain is getting worse or not improving after two weeks, or if moving your arm is very difficult. Get urgent help for any of these:

- sudden or severe pain, or an arm you cannot move
- a change in the shoulder's shape, or a lot of swelling
- numbness or pins and needles that do not go away
- pain after a fall or injury

## When is your pillow not the cause of neck pain?

When the pain builds during the day, follows an injury or comes with other symptoms, look beyond the pillow.

Mayo Clinic lists strained muscles, worn joints, pressure on nerves and injuries, such as falls and car accidents, among the causes. A pillow can reduce strain at night, but it does not address any of these.

- **Desk and phone posture.** Long hours looking down at a screen load the neck. Read [what tech neck is and what helps](/blog/what-is-tech-neck-and-how-can-a-pillow-help-fix-it-in-singapore), and try our [posture habits and exercises](/blog/improve-good-posture-habits-with-exercises-and-home-remedies).
- **Your mattress.** A sagging mattress can tilt your neck even on a well-fitted pillow, so check it too.
- **An injury.** Pain after a car accident or a fall needs a medical check, not a new pillow.

Your pillow is worth checking first if your neck is stiffest when you wake. The same goes if the pain began with a new pillow, or eases when you sleep elsewhere.

Loud snoring, gasping or choking at night and feeling very tired during the day can be signs of sleep apnoea. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sleep-apnoea/) advises seeing a GP about them. If you already use CPAP, our page on [pillows for CPAP users](/blog/how-a-customised-pillow-can-help-with-sleep-apnea-for-cpap-users) explains what a pillow can and cannot do.

## When should you see a doctor about neck or shoulder pain?

See a doctor or physiotherapist if neck pain is not easing after a few weeks, keeps coming back, or stops you sleeping or working. HealthHub Singapore says most neck pain improves within about six weeks, and advises seeing a doctor if it gets worse.

Based on advice from the [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/neck-pain-and-stiff-neck/) and Mayo Clinic, see a GP or physiotherapist soon if you have:

- neck pain that is getting worse, or has not improved with self-care
- pain that spreads down your arm
- numbness, tingling or pins and needles in your arms or hands

Get urgent help, at A&E or by calling 995, if:

- severe neck pain follows an injury, such as a car crash, diving accident or fall
- severe neck pain comes with a high fever
- you have weakness in an arm or leg, or trouble walking

[Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/symptoms/neck-pain/basics/when-to-see-doctor/sym-20050882) lists these as signs that need emergency care. The [SCDF](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/home/about-scdf/emergency-medical-services) lists major trauma among the emergencies to call 995 for.

A pillow is for comfort and support. It does not replace a diagnosis.

## Frequently asked questions

### What pillow height is best for neck pain?

There is no single right height. Side sleepers need enough height to keep the head level above the shoulder, so broader shoulders need a higher pillow. Back sleepers need a lower pillow that supports the curve of the neck.

### Can a pillow cause shoulder pain?

It can contribute. A pillow too low for side sleeping lets your head drop, and many people with one sore shoulder sleep on that side. Change sides, check your pillow height, and see a GP if the pain lasts over two weeks.

### Is memory foam good for neck pain?

Memory foam can work if its height suits your build and sleep position. Its drawback for a sore neck is that a solid foam block has one fixed height, which cannot be adjusted if it is wrong.

### Is it better to sleep on your back or side with neck pain?

Both can work with the right pillow height, and they are the two positions Mpillow recommends. Sleeping on your stomach keeps your neck turned for hours, so it is the position to move away from.

### How do I know if my pillow is too high?

On your side, your head tilts up towards the ceiling. On your back, your chin is pushed towards your chest.
