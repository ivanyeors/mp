What is the best pillow for neck pain?

The best pillow for neck pain is one that supports the natural curve of your cervical spine, keeps your neck aligned with your shoulders, and suits your sleep position and body build.

Why do many people in Singapore suffer from neck pain during sleep?

Common reasons include:

Prolonged screen time causing tech neck
Using pillows that are too high or too flat
Hot and humid climate causing discomfort and poor sleep quality
One-size-fits-all pillows that don’t suit individual body types


What pillow features help relieve neck pain?

Look for a pillow that offers:

Customisable height and firmness
Cervical neck support
Proper support for both side and back sleeping
Breathable, washable materials for hygiene


Is a customised pillow better for neck pain than a regular pillow?

Yes. A customised pillow allows you to:

Adjust firmness to your comfort level
Maintain correct neck alignment
Reduce pressure on neck and shoulders
Adapt the pillow as your sleeping needs change

Office workers and heavy screen users
