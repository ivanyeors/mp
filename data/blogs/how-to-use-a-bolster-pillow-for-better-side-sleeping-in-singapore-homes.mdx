What is a bolster pillow used for?

A bolster pillow is designed to:

Support the body while sleeping
Maintain proper spinal alignment
Reduce pressure on the shoulders and hips
Improve comfort for side sleepers

Unlike flat pillows, bolsters provide structured support along the body.

Why are bolsters helpful for side sleepers?

Side sleepers benefit because bolsters:

Prevent the upper body from collapsing forward
Keep the spine neutral
Support the arms and shoulders
Reduce tossing and turning

Without support, side sleepers often experience neck, shoulder, and lower back pain.

How should you position a bolster for side sleeping?

For best results:

Hug the bolster in front of your chest
Place it between your knees
Keep your head aligned with your neck
Relax your shoulders around the bolster

This keeps your spine straight from neck to hips.

What size bolster should you use in Singapore?

Choose based on height:

Small: Children or below 1.50m
Medium: 1.50m–1.80m
Large: Above 1.80m

Correct sizing ensures your body stays supported without strain.

What features make a good bolster pillow?

A good bolster should offer:

Adjustable firmness
Modular fillings
Breathable, washable materials
Sizes to match body build
Hygiene for humid Singapore homes

Who should use a bolster pillow?

Bolsters are suitable for:

Side sleepers
People with shoulder or hip pain
Pregnant sleepers
Anyone seeking deeper, more stable sleep
