You have a pillow you like, a bolster you have slept with since childhood, and an advert telling you to buy a body pillow. It is fair to wonder which of them is doing anything. This page compares the three, shows how to sleep with a body pillow properly, and helps you decide whether you need more than one. For size, firmness and washing advice, see our [guide to choosing a bolster](/blog/the-complete-guide-to-choosing-the-right-bolster-in-singapore).

## What is the difference between a pillow, a bolster and a body pillow?

A pillow supports your head and neck. A bolster and a body pillow support your body, mainly your top arm and your legs when you lie on your side. The difference between the last two is mostly size and shape.

| Option | Shape | What it supports | Best for | Bed space | Washing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular pillow | Flat or contoured, head-sized | Head and neck | Everyone | Small | Depends on the filling |
| Bolster | Long, round cylinder | Top arm, chest and knees | Side sleepers; back sleepers, under the knees | Moderate | Depends on the build; modular ones come apart |
| Body pillow | Long and flatter; straight, C- or U-shaped | Front, legs, and sometimes the back | Side sleepers who want more coverage; pregnancy | Large, especially C and U shapes | Often too bulky to wash whole at home |

None of the three replaces the others. A bolster or body pillow cannot hold your head at the right height, and a head pillow cannot reach your knees.

## What does a regular pillow do?

A pillow keeps your head in line with your spine, so your neck is not bent up or down for hours. The height you need depends on how you sleep. For side sleepers, [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/lower-back-pain-in-the-morning) suggests a thick pillow that keeps the top of the spine straight and parallel to the mattress. On your back, there is no shoulder width to fill, so you need less height.

This is why the Mpillow's height is set from your shoulder length. On your side, your shoulder sets how far your head sits from the mattress. The Mpillow also has three zones: a centre zone for back sleeping, and left and right zones at different heights for side sleeping. A bolster cannot do this job. Resting your head on a narrow, round cushion gives your neck an unsteady surface that rolls as you move.

## What does a bolster do that a pillow can't?

A bolster supports the parts of your body a head pillow cannot reach. Hugged in front, it gives your top arm a rest. Tucked between your knees, it keeps your legs apart and your hips level.

On your side, your top knee tends to drop forward onto the mattress and pull your pelvis and lower back round with it. [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852) recommends a pillow between the knees, which can help align the spine, pelvis and hips.

On your back, Mayo Clinic says a pillow under your knees may help your back muscles relax and keep the curve of your lower back. Mpillow gives back sleepers similar advice: you may need support for your lower back.

## How do you sleep correctly with a body pillow?

Lie on your side, hug the top of the body pillow under your top arm, and bring its lower half between your knees. Keep your head on a separate pillow, at a height that keeps your neck level. The same steps work with a long bolster.

1. **Start on your side** with your knees slightly bent.
2. **Hug the top end at chest height,** so your top arm and shoulder rest on it instead of dropping forward.
3. **Bring the lower end between your knees,** and between your ankles if it is long enough, so your top leg stays level with your hip.
4. **Keep your head on its own pillow.** The body pillow should not prop up your head.
5. **Check the line.** Your ear, shoulder and hip should sit roughly in a straight line, with no twist at the waist.

### If your body pillow is C- or U-shaped

The back section stops you rolling onto your back during the night. A U shape also lets you turn from one side to the other without moving the pillow. Both take up far more of the bed than a straight pillow.

### If you are trying to stop sleeping on your stomach

A body pillow along your front gives some of the pressure on the stomach that stomach sleepers are used to, while keeping you on your side. [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/is-your-pillow-hurting-your-neck-7-tips-for-better-sleep) suggests body pillows to habitual stomach sleepers for this reason. Body pillow mistakes are much the same as bolster mistakes: too thick between the knees, too soft, hugged too high, or used under your head. ## Do you need both a pillow and a bolster?

If you sleep on your side, usually yes. The pillow looks after your neck, and the bolster looks after your arm, hips and lower back. If you sleep on your back without pain, a pillow alone may be enough, with a bolster under your knees as an option.

| How you sleep | Pillow | Bolster or body pillow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side | Yes, with enough height for your shoulder width | Recommended, hugged and between the knees |
| Back | Yes, lower than a side sleeper needs | Optional, under the knees |
| Side and back | Yes, ideally lower in the centre and higher at the sides | Useful for the time you spend on your side |
| Stomach | Mpillow suggests trying to move to your side | Can help you settle on your side |
| Later pregnancy | Yes | Often used for side sleeping |

## Should you choose a bolster or a body pillow?

Choose a bolster if you want something compact that is easy to wash and leaves room in a shared bed. Choose a body pillow if you want support along your whole front, and sometimes your back, which some people prefer in pregnancy.

Several practical differences matter in Singapore homes:

- **Space:** a C- or U-shaped body pillow can take over much of a bed. In a compact HDB bedroom, or a bed you share, a bolster leaves room for a partner.
- **Washing:** large body pillows are often too bulky for a home washing machine. In humid weather, something you can wash fully is easier to keep fresh.
- **Warmth:** a pillow that wraps around you covers more of your skin, which some people find too warm on a hot night.
- **Adjustability:** most bolsters and body pillows have a fixed fill. A modular bolster such as the Mbolster lets you adjust its firmness through its fillings, and the fillings come out so it can be machine-washed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a body pillow the same as a bolster?

No. A bolster is a round cushion that you hug or tuck between your knees. A body pillow is longer and flatter, and is often C- or U-shaped to wrap around you.

### Can a body pillow replace my regular pillow?

No. A body pillow supports your body, not your head. Your neck still needs its own pillow at the right height for how you sleep.

### Can I use a bolster if I sleep on my back?

Yes. A bolster under your knees may help your lower back relax. Mpillow advises that back sleepers may need some support for the lower back.

### Which takes up less space, a bolster or a body pillow?

A straight bolster. C- and U-shaped body pillows take up far more of the bed, which matters in a compact HDB bedroom or a bed you share.
