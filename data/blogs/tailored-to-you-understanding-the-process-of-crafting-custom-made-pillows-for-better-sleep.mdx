You may have bought pillow after pillow and still woken with a stiff neck, because each one was made for an average body. A custom pillow starts from the other end: your measurements first, the pillow second.

This page explains what "custom" should mean for a pillow, then walks through how Mpillow fits one, from the first measurement to re-adjustment. For a shorter summary, see our [custom pillow guide](/guides/custom-pillow).

## What makes a pillow custom?

A pillow is custom when someone measures you and sets its height, shape and firmness from those numbers and how you sleep. A real fit can also be changed later. A pillow picked from three firmness levels is a choice, not a fit.

The word is used loosely, so it helps to sort pillows by who sets the height, and from what.

| Pillow type | Who sets the height | Based on | Can it change later? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard pillow | The factory | An average sleeper | No |
| Firmness options (soft, medium, firm) | You, by picking one | Preference | No |
| Adjustable-fill pillow | You, by adding or removing fill | Trial and error | Yes, by you |
| Contour pillow in sizes | You, from a size chart | One or two measurements | No |
| Made-to-measure modular pillow | A fitter, from your measurements | Several measurements and how you sleep | Yes, by re-adjustment |

Three questions separate a fitted pillow from a marketing claim:

1. **Was it measured?** Something about your body, at the least your shoulder width, should set the height.
2. **Does it fit how you sleep?** Side and back sleeping need different heights, so a combination sleeper needs both in one pillow.
3. **Can it change?** Bodies, mattresses and habits change. A fitted pillow should be adjustable without buying a new one.

## Why can't one pillow height suit everyone?

One height cannot suit everyone because the gap a pillow has to fill depends on your body. Shoulder width sets most of it for side sleepers. Neck length, the curve of the neck and the shape of the head change it further.

Studies of pillow height point the same way. A 2021 review found that body size is one of the main factors in the right height. No single range could yet be identified as best. [[cite: Lei et al. 2021 Healthcare review: body dimension is a determinant of pillow height; optimal range cannot yet be identified | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8544534/]] Another 2021 review concluded that shape and height affect neck alignment more than the filling material. [[cite: Pang et al. 2021 Clinical Biomechanics systematic review: cervical alignment impacted by pillow shape and height rather than material | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33895703/]]

The Mpillow is designed around this. The length of your shoulders sets the pillow's height. Mpillow has developed more than 50 configurations. They cover round or flat heads, curved or straight spines, long or short necks, and thick or flat backs.

### Step 2: Tell us about yourself

The configurator opens with a short "what to expect" screen: about you, measurements, then your results. It begins by asking your name, gender and age group.

### Step 3: Take the measurements

The Premium configurator guides you through four measurements, in inches by default, with a switch to centimetres.

- **Shoulder length:** the width across your shoulders. This sets the pillow's size and height.
- **Neck to back:** standing with your back against a wall, the distance from the top of your neck to the wall.
- **Head to back:** from the back of your head to your upper back, also measured against the wall.
- **Neck length:** measured straight up, from the top of your shoulders to the top of your neck.

The Basic configurator asks for shoulder length only, and uses it to choose your size. If you fall between two sizes, round up.

Each step shows you how to take the measurement. If you are unsure you got one right, you can continue with your best estimate and refine the fit later through a re-adjustment.

### Step 4: Answer a few more questions

Measurements describe your body; the next questions describe your nights. The Premium configurator asks about:

- your sleeper type: side and back, side only, back only, or stomach;
- whether you use a bolster;
- how firm your bed is;
- whether your ear feels uncomfortable when you sleep on your side;
- any pain or sleep issues: neck, shoulder, lower back, sleep apnoea or poor sleep quality;
- acid reflux and blood pressure.

Every question needs an answer. You can also add front and side photos, taken in a fitted shirt, before you enter your contact details. The photos are optional.

### Step 5: See your recommendation

The configurator then shows a recommended size, with a plain-language explanation of why. This is where you choose the parts that come down to taste: the cover and the case, plus any add-ons and your shipping.

You do not pick the fillings yourself. The protector comes with your tier, and the filling mix comes from a recipe our team confirms for your build.

When you submit, we place an authorisation hold instead of charging you. Our team then assigns your size, tier and filling mix. That mix combines softer hollow-fibre pouches and firmer fibre-ball pouches, placed in the pillow's left, centre and right compartments.

If a part you chose is out of stock, your submission becomes a pre-order, and our team will contact you on WhatsApp. The configurator may also offer a switch between Basic and Premium; nothing changes unless you agree.

### Step 7: Review the final build before you pay

You see the final build before any payment is captured. Only when you accept it is payment taken, and production follows.

## What can a custom pillow do for neck pain, and what can't it?

A custom pillow can take the guesswork out of height and firmness, so the pillow is not adding strain while you sleep. It will not resolve a neck condition on its own, and it is no substitute for being assessed.

Trials of pillows for neck pain are few and small. A 2021 review found that some pillow designs reduced neck pain and waking symptoms in people with chronic neck pain. Pillow design made no difference to their sleep quality. [[cite: Pang et al. 2021: spring and rubber pillows reduced neck pain and waking symptoms in chronic neck pain; pillow design did not influence sleep quality | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33895703/]]

See a doctor or physiotherapist if neck pain lasts more than a few weeks or keeps returning. Do the same after an injury, or if you have numbness, tingling or weakness in your arms or hands. [[cite: NHS neck pain: see a GP if pain does not improve after a few weeks, or with pins and needles, numbness or weakness | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/neck-pain/]]

## Is a custom pillow worth it?

A custom pillow is worth it if standard pillows have not worked for you. It sets the height from your body instead of guessing. If a regular pillow already lets you wake comfortable, keep it.

You are most likely to notice the difference if:

- you sleep on both your side and your back, and need two heights in one pillow;
- your shoulders are broad or narrow enough that standard heights miss;
- you have a more curved neck profile, which the Premium Cervical Neck Support accessory is designed for;
- your pillows flatten within months, and you keep buying new ones.

A modular pillow also changes what "wearing out" means. Worn pouches can be topped up or replaced at a re-adjustment, instead of the whole pillow going in the bin.

### What is the difference between a custom pillow and an adjustable pillow?

An adjustable pillow lets you add or remove filling yourself, by trial and error. A custom pillow is built from your measurements and how you sleep, then re-adjusted by a fitter if needed.
