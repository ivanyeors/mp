A pillow of the wrong height or shape can leave you waking with a stiff neck and sore shoulders. That was the problem we set out to explain when Mr Poon of KH Poon Physiotherapy joined us for an hour-long video call in 2020.

This page is our short summary of that video call. It is written by Mpillow and sets out the points we made about the Mpillow, so it does not quote Mr Poon. For a wider look at choosing a pillow when your neck hurts, see our [guide to the best pillow for neck pain](/blog/what-is-the-best-pillow-for-neck-pain-in-singapore).

## Why does the shape of a pillow matter?

The shape of a pillow is what makes it comfortable, and it was the first benefit we discussed. The Mpillow's M shape can look odd at first sight, but it came out of the study we did before designing the pillow.

The M shape lets one pillow support you both on your side and on your back. Inside, the Mpillow is divided into three zones:

- **The centre zone** is for back sleeping and supports the curve of the neck.
- **The left and right zones** are for side sleeping, and sit at different heights.

## How do modular fillings fit the Mpillow to your body?

Modular fillings let the Mpillow be fitted to the person sleeping on it, which was the second benefit. We made small filling packs that sit in the pillow's three compartments. They build the height you need on your side and the curved support you need on your back.

In the session we explained that fabric plays only a small part in good sleep. What matters most is ergonomics: whether the pillow's shape and height suit your body structure.

A conventional pillow is made to a single profile, but bodies differ. The same pillow can be too high for one person and too low for another, too soft for some and too firm for others. In our experience, many adjustable pillows are messy to adjust, with no way to measure how much change you need.

So many people end up trying to fit their body to a pillow. They spend a lot of money and collect a pile of pillows on the bed. With the Mpillow we set out to do the reverse: fit the pillow to the person, then make it easy to adjust.

We see that modular build as what sets the Mpillow apart. Each pillow is made of layers that can be customised and adjusted for one individual. Our aim is to help people solve their pillow problems, sleep better and keep a good sleeping posture for the neck and shoulders.

## How do you keep the Mpillow clean?

Every part of the Mpillow comes apart and can be washed, which was the third benefit. Hygiene was part of the design from the start, because a conventional pillow is usually one piece and its filling is hard to clean.

The Mpillow's separate parts each protect the fillings and wash on their own:

- **The case** is shaped to the M design, with an anti-slip layer underneath, so a normal pillowcase will not fit over it.
- **The cover or cover sheets** come off and wash like your clothes, as often as you like.
- **The waterproof inner protector** on the Premium Mpillow keeps liquids away from the fillings, with filter holes that let air escape. Wash it about every six months.
- **The filling packs** lift out, so the whole pillow can be taken apart and cleaned.

The [Mpillow aftercare video](/mp-care) shows how to wash and clean the pillow step by step.

## How do I know which Mpillow size suits me?

Your Mpillow size comes from one measurement: your shoulder length. Mpillow's online configurators show you how to take it at home.

In the session we explained why. Your shoulder length sets the height the pillow must reach when you lie on your side. That height is the main difference between the sizes. A pillow that is too high or too low tilts your head out of line with your spine. That holds on your side and on your back.

Our guide to [Mpillow sizes and configurations](/blog/which-mpillow-configuration-is-for-you) has the full size chart. Neck pain that persists, or comes with numbness, tingling or weakness, needs a doctor or physiotherapist, not just a new pillow.

### Can I sleep on my stomach with the Mpillow?

Mpillow recommends sleeping on your side or your back, the two positions the pillow is designed for. If you usually sleep on your stomach, Mpillow suggests trying to sleep on your side instead.
