We had a zoom talk with the founder of KHPoon Physiotherapy, Mr. Poon to have a chat with Mpillow regarding getting proper sleep and to discuss the top 3 benefits of having a good pillow. 
ShapeThe secret to having a good sleep, or that makes a pillow comfortable is the shape of the pillow. It might look like an odd shape when you see it at first, however, this is based on the study we made before design our pillow.
This pillow allows you to sleep on both the side and on the back comfortably! Secondly, we made mini pillow fillings packs to be arranged in our 3 compartment pillow sections to create the height of the pillow when sleep on the side, and curvature support when sleeping on the back.You can refer to them here:
It is mainly not the type of fabric that lets you have a good sleep, but mainly the ergonomics of the pillow that suits your body structure. Types of fabric only play a small part in this.
If you compare traditional pillows, they do not give you the support you actually need due to the difference in body profiles. For some people, it might be too high to sleep on, for some its too low, some too soft and some too firm. The pillows that are adjustable in the market are messy, and could not be measured to how much adjustments are needed.

Many of them ended up trying to suit their body structure to a pillow they are finding, which in the end spending tons of money, with tons of pillows on the bed. For Mpillow, we want to make it a point to suit our pillow to our clients. And to make it better, they can have the flexibility to adjust it themselves easily.

It is our mission to help people solve their pillow problems and sleep issues the best we can. Mpillow is just a product not just to help, but to also optimise sleeping posture to prevent future neck aches and shoulder pains. 


I think one unique way of how we design Mpillow with other brands is the modularity of the product. It all ties back to customising and adjusting each pillow for every single individual that wants a customised pillow. So we made a pillow that has many different layers of modularity, customizability and flexibility to adjust. There is no pillow out there that could tailor their pillow to suit every single individual.

How do I know what pillow size is suitable for me?All you need to do is just to measure your shoulder length, from the tip of your shoulder bone to the other tip of your shoulder bone. So the difference with the sizes is where you get comfort when you sleep.
The Length of one shoulder is the Height in which you need when sleeping on the side. That is the main difference. Too high or too low will cause strains on your neck.It is also the same for the back as well.
