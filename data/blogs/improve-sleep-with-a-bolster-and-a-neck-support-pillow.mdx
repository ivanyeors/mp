Getting a good night’s sleep is essential for overall well-being, and the right sleep accessories can make all the difference. If you often wake up with neck stiffness or discomfort, using both a bolster and a neck support pillow can help improve your sleep posture and relieve tension.
Why Use a Neck Support Pillow?
A neck support pillow is designed to keep your head and neck in proper alignment while you sleep. Unlike regular pillows, it provides targeted support to reduce pressure on the neck muscles and maintain the natural curve of your spine. Whether you are a side or back sleeper, a well-designed neck support pillow can help prevent stiffness and discomfort, ensuring a more restful sleep.
How a Bolster Enhances Comfort
A bolster is a long, cylindrical pillow that provides additional support for different parts of the body. When used alongside a neck support pillow, it enhances comfort in several ways:
For side sleepers: Placing a bolster between the knees helps align the spine, reducing strain on the neck and shoulders.
For back sleepers: A bolster under the knees eases lower back tension, improving overall spinal alignment and indirectly relieving neck discomfort.
For extra neck support: Some people use a small bolster under their neck to complement their neck support pillow for added comfort.
Customising Your Sleep Setup
The key to better sleep is finding the right combination of support. A customisable bolster allows you to adjust its firmness to suit your preference, while a neck support pillow ensures proper alignment for your head and neck. Investing in both can significantly improve your sleep quality, helping you wake up feeling refreshed and pain-free.
