If you’re a side sleeper, finding the right support can make a huge difference in how well you rest at night. That’s where Mbolster—our innovative, customisable bolster—comes in. Designed to complement your sleep posture and pair perfectly with the Mpillow, Mbolster offers comfort, support, and flexibility like no other.
Traditional bolsters are often a one-size-fits-all solution, but the Mbolster breaks that mold. It comes in three different sizes, making it easy to choose the one that best matches your height and sleeping preference. Whether you like to hug your bolster, tuck it between your knees, or use it for added back support, there’s an Mbolster that’s just right for you.
One of the standout features of the Mbolster is its adjustable firmness. You can remove or add filling to match your comfort level. For side sleepers, this means you can achieve the ideal support for your knees and hips, helping to maintain a healthy spinal alignment throughout the night. Proper alignment can reduce joint pressure, prevent back strain, and even improve circulation while you sleep.
Not only is it adjustable, but the Mbolster is also machine washable inside and out—a feature that’s rare in traditional bolsters. This makes it a more hygienic option and easier to maintain over time.
As a sleep companion, the Mbolster is a perfect match for the Mpillow. While Mpillow supports your neck and shoulders, the Mbolster helps complete your sleep system by providing balance and alignment for the lower half of your body.
Whether you’re looking for a better bolster for side sleeping or simply want to enhance your comfort, the Mbolster is the ideal solution—tailored, supportive, and designed with your sleep health in mind.
