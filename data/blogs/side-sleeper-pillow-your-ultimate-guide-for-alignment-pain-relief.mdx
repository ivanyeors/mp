Side sleepers often wake with a sore shoulder, a numb arm, or a neck that complains at the first turn of the head. When that happens night after night, the pillow is one of the first things worth checking.

This guide covers pillow height and firmness for side sleepers, knee and arm support, ear comfort, and sleeping in more than one position. Back and stomach sleepers get a short section each. It is part of our guide to [choosing the best pillow in Singapore](/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-pillow-in-singapore).

## What does a side sleeper's pillow need to do?

A side sleeper's pillow has to fill the gap that your shoulder creates between your head and the mattress. Done right, your head neither tips down towards the mattress nor is propped up towards the ceiling.

Adults spend more of the night on their side than in any other position. In one study using body-worn sensors, people spent about 54% of their time in bed on their side. [[cite: Skarpsno et al. 2017 Nature and Science of Sleep: participants spent about 54% of time in bed in the side position | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5677378]] It is also the position that asks most of a pillow, because the shoulder holds your upper body off the bed.

A quick check: lie on your side and ask someone to look at you from the foot of the bed. Your nose should line up with the centre of your chest, along a line parallel to the mattress.

## How high should a pillow be for side sleeping?

A side sleeper's pillow should be about as high as the distance from the side of your neck to the edge of your shoulder. Subtract however far your shoulder sinks into the mattress. That is why shoulder width matters more than any general size.

| Factor | You need more height if… | You need less height if… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shoulder width | Your shoulders are broad | Your shoulders are narrow |
| Mattress | It is firm, so your shoulder stays up | It is soft, so your shoulder sinks in |
| Filling | It is soft and compresses under your head | It is firm and keeps its shape |

A pillow is too low if you slide an arm under it, fold it in half, or wake with your head tipped down. If your head is tilted up and the side of your neck feels stretched, the pillow is too high.

Research has not pinned down one ideal pillow height, partly because body size varies so much. [[cite: Lei et al. 2021 Healthcare review: body dimension is a determinant of pillow height; optimal range for side-lying cannot yet be identified | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8544534/]] At Mpillow, the length of your shoulders sets the pillow's height. ## How firm should a side sleeper's pillow be?

A side sleeper's pillow should be medium-firm to firm, so that it holds its height under the weight of your head all night. The surface can still feel soft.

Fillings that hold height well include latex, foam, buckwheat, and fibre packed into separate compartments or pouches. Loose polyester and down compress the most, so they suit side sleepers least. In one trial of side sleepers, a latex pillow did best for waking symptoms and a feather pillow did worst. [[cite: Gordon et al. 2010 J Pain Res field trial in side sleepers: latex performed best, feather least well for waking symptoms | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3004642/]]

## What should side sleepers do with their knees, arms and shoulders?

Support your top leg and top arm, so that your body does not roll forward. A pillow or bolster between the knees stops the top leg dropping across, which keeps the hips and lower back level. [[cite: side sleepers: knees slightly bent with a pillow between them helps align spine, pelvis and hips | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852]]

- **Knees:** bend them slightly and keep a pillow, or the lower end of a bolster, between them.
- **Top arm:** hug a bolster or pillow, so the top shoulder does not roll forward and down.
- **Lower arm:** keep it in front of you rather than trapped under the pillow.
- **A sore shoulder:** try sleeping on the other side. In a study of people with pain in one shoulder, most of those who slept on one side lay on the painful shoulder. [[cite: Kempf and Kongsted 2012: 67% of one-sided sleepers with unilateral shoulder pain lay on the painful side; association, causality unknown | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22608285/]]

## Why does your ear hurt when you sleep on your side?

Your ear is pressed between your head and the pillow, so a firm, high pillow can push on it for hours. A pillow at the right height spreads the weight across the side of your head, and a softer top layer eases the pressure.

The Premium Mpillow configurator asks whether you get ear discomfort when sleeping on your side, alongside your measurements. Mpillow also offers an Ear Comfort Pad.

If an ear stays sore after you change your pillow, see a doctor. Get a tender lump on the ear's rim checked, especially if lying on it hurts, because it can look like a skin cancer. [[cite: chondrodermatitis nodularis helicis: painful ear nodule, worse lying on that side; can resemble skin cancer, so needs medical assessment | https://www.ccjm.org/content/90/5/277.full.pdf]]

## Which side should you sleep on?

Either side can work, but acid reflux and pregnancy change the advice. Sleeping on the left side is linked with less night-time acid reflux than sleeping on the right. [[cite: 2023 meta-analysis: left lateral sleep decreased acid exposure and clearance time compared with right lateral and supine | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10643078/]]

In later pregnancy, the advice is to go to sleep on your side rather than your back. [[cite: third trimester: go to sleep on your side, not your back | https://www.tommys.org/pregnancy-information/im-pregnant/sleep-position-pregnancy-qa]] Our guide to [pregnancy bolster pillows](/blog/why-a-pregnancy-bolster-pillow-is-essential-for-back-and-hip-pain-relief) covers support for the bump, hips and back.

## What if you sleep on your side and your back?

Combination sleepers need two heights in one pillow: lower in the middle for back sleeping, and higher at the edges for side sleeping. One even height will be too low when you are on your side, or too high when you are on your back.

An adjustable pillow set to a compromise height partly suits both. A zoned pillow gives each position its own height, so you can roll over without rearranging the pillow. ## What pillow do back sleepers need?

Back sleepers need a medium-height pillow that fills the hollow behind the neck without pushing the head forward. A pillow slightly higher under the neck than under the head supports that curve. [[cite: back sleepers: rounded support under the neck curve with a flatter area under the head | https://www.health.harvard.edu/pain/say-good-night-to-neck-pain]]

- **Too high:** your chin is pushed towards your chest.
- **Too low:** your head tips back and your chin points upwards.
- **Lower back:** Mpillow's own advice is that back sleepers may also need lower-back support. A pillow under the knees is one way to give it. [[cite: back sleepers: a pillow under the knees may help maintain the curve of the lower back | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852]]

If you snore, trying to sleep on your side may help. [[cite: NHS snoring: try sleeping on your side | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/snoring/]] See a doctor if you gasp or choke in your sleep, or feel very sleepy in the day. These can be signs of sleep apnoea. [[cite: NHS snoring: see a GP if gasping or choking at night or sleepy in the day, possible sleep apnoea | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/snoring/]]

## What pillow do stomach sleepers need?

Stomach sleepers need the thinnest, softest pillow they can manage, or none. Face down, you have to turn your head fully to one side to breathe, and a thick pillow tilts it back as well. [[cite: stomach sleeping requires turning the head to one side, which strains the neck | https://health.clevelandclinic.org/sleeping-on-stomach]]

Mpillow recommends that stomach sleepers try moving to side sleeping. If you stay on your front, a thin pillow under your hips and lower stomach can ease strain on your lower back. [[cite: stomach sleepers: a pillow under the hips and lower abdomen can reduce back strain | https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852]] Our guide to [using a bolster for better sleep](/blog/how-to-use-a-bolster-pillow-for-better-side-sleeping-in-singapore-homes) shows a set-up with two Mbolsters for stomach sleepers.

## When should a side sleeper see a doctor or physiotherapist?

See a doctor or physiotherapist if neck or shoulder pain lasts more than a few weeks or keeps coming back. Do the same after an injury, or if you have numbness, tingling or weakness in your arm or hand. [[cite: NHS neck pain: see a GP if pain does not improve after a few weeks, or with pins and needles, numbness or weakness | https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/neck-pain/]] A better pillow can reduce strain at night, but it cannot heal an injury or free a trapped nerve.

## Frequently asked questions

### How high should a pillow be for a side sleeper?

High enough to fill the space between your ear and the outside of your shoulder, so your head stays level with your spine. Broader shoulders and firmer mattresses need more height.

### Should side sleepers use a firm or soft pillow?

Medium-firm to firm, so the pillow keeps its height under your head all night. A soft surface layer is fine, as long as the pillow underneath does not collapse.

### Is memory foam good for side sleepers?

A memory foam pillow holds its height well, but a solid block comes in one fixed height and can feel warm. It suits a side sleeper only if that height matches their shoulders.

### Is it better to sleep on your left or right side?

Either side suits most people. If you have acid reflux, sleeping on your left side is linked with less night-time reflux than sleeping on your right.

### Can one pillow work for side and back sleeping?

Yes, if it gives two heights: higher at the edges for side sleeping and lower in the centre for back sleeping. The Mpillow's M shape is designed this way.

### Why does my ear hurt when I sleep on my side?

Your ear is pressed between your head and the pillow, often for hours. A pillow at the right height with a softer surface spreads the pressure. See a doctor if the ear stays sore or you find a tender lump.
