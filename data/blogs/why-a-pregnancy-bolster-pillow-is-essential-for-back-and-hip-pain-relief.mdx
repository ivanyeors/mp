Pregnancy is a special time, but it often comes with discomfort—especially when it comes to sleep. Many expecting mothers struggle to find a comfortable position at night. That’s where a pregnancy bolster pillow becomes a game-changer. Designed to provide full-body support, the bolster for pregnancy is an excellent solution for relieving back, hip, and leg pain during sleep.
How a Bolster Pillow for Back Pain Helps During Pregnancy
As your belly grows, sleeping on your back becomes difficult and even discouraged. Side sleeping is recommended, but it can put strain on your hips and lower back without proper support. A bolster pillow for back pain helps support your body by maintaining healthy alignment between your spine, hips, and knees.
By placing a pregnancy bolster pillow between your legs while sleeping on your side, you can reduce hip pressure and keep your spine neutral. Many moms-to-be also enjoy hugging a bolster pillow, which reduces tension in the shoulders and improves upper body posture.
