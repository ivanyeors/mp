## What is a bolster, and why do so many Singapore homes have one?

A bolster is a long, round cushion that you hug or lay along your body while you sleep. In Singapore, many people grow up sleeping with one, and the habit often stays with them into adulthood.

A bolster is not the same as a body pillow. Body pillows are usually longer and flatter, and some are bent into a C or U shape. A regular pillow does a third job: it holds up your head. We compare all three in [bolster vs body pillow vs pillow](/blog/bolsters-vs-pillows-do-you-really-need-both).

People keep a bolster for two kinds of reason. Some want physical support when they lie on their side. Others simply settle better with something to hold. Both are fair reasons, and a well-chosen bolster serves both.

## What does a bolster actually do for your body?

A bolster mainly helps side sleepers hold a steady, level position. Hugged in front, it gives your top arm somewhere to rest. Tucked between your knees, it stops your top leg sliding forward and twisting your lower back.

### Between the knees

When you lie on your side, your top knee tends to drop onto the mattress in front of you. That pulls your pelvis round and twists your lower spine. [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/back-pain/in-depth/sleeping-positions/art-20546852) advises side sleepers to draw their knees up slightly and put a pillow between them. This can help align the spine, pelvis and hips, and a full-length body pillow is another option.

### Hugged in front of the chest

Without support, your top arm falls forward across your body and your shoulder rolls with it. Resting that arm on something thick in front of you keeps it closer to level. For side sleepers with a sore shoulder, [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/relieve-rotator-cuff-pain-night) suggests building a "pillow wall" in front of you to rest your arm on.

### What a bolster does not do

A bolster does not support your neck. Your neck depends on your head pillow filling the gap between your ear and the mattress. On your side, that gap is set mostly by the width of your shoulders. Choose a bolster that matches your height. It should be long enough to hug at chest height and still reach between your knees, without hanging off the bed. Too short, and you must choose between supporting your arm and supporting your knees. Too long, and it crowds a shared bed.

## How firm should a bolster be?

A bolster should be firm enough to keep its shape under your arm and between your knees, and soft enough to hug without effort. If it flattens overnight, your knees end up touching by morning. If it is too firm or too thick, it props your top arm and knee too high, and holding it feels like hugging a log.

The right firmness depends on where the bolster sits:

- **Between the knees:** it should hold your top knee roughly level with your hip. A thin or soft bolster lets the knee drop. An overly thick one lifts it and tilts your pelvis the other way.
- **Hugged at the chest:** most people prefer a softer feel here, as long as it still holds the arm up.
- **Both at once:** a single bolster that does both jobs needs an even fill along its length, so neither end sags.

Firmness also changes with time. A bolster with a fixed fill tends to soften and go uneven as the filling compacts. Once it has flattened, there is little you can do to restore it.

## How do you keep a bolster clean in Singapore's humid weather?

Wash the case with the rest of your bed linen, and wash the inner and filling regularly. Singapore is humid all year round, so dry everything completely before you put it back together.

A bolster you hug sits against your skin all night and absorbs sweat. House dust mites prefer warm, humid conditions. An NHS trust, [Cambridge University Hospitals](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/dust-mites-in-your-home/), advises washing bedding on a 60°C cycle to reduce dust mites. It notes that a cooler wash only partly kills them.

A simple routine:

1. **Case:** wash it with your pillowcases and sheets.
2. **Inner and filling:** wash them when they smell stale or look discoloured, and more often if anyone at home has a dust mite allergy.
3. **Drying:** dry everything fully in a well-ventilated spot. In humid air, thick fillings take longer to dry than you might expect.
4. **Replacing:** if an old bolster has gone yellow, lumpy or musty inside and cannot be washed whole, it is time for a new one.

This is where a bolster's build matters. Many traditional bolsters cannot be washed whole, so only the case ever gets cleaned. The Mbolster's fillings come out, so the bolster can go in the washing machine, and Mpillow describes it as 100% washable. Each Mbolster comes with a laundry bag and a vacuum bag.

## Can the whole family use a bolster?

Yes, as long as each person has a size that suits them. The Mbolster is sized by height for the whole household, from children through to adults. Size S suits children and people up to 1.50 m, and sizes M and L cover taller children and adults.

A few points for families:

- **Babies:** never put a bolster, pillow or other soft item in a baby's cot. For a baby's first year, the [American Academy of Pediatrics](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/a-parents-guide-to-safe-sleep.aspx) advises a firm mattress with a snugly fitted sheet, and nothing else.
- **Growing children:** check a child's bolster size against their height every so often, and move up a size when they pass 1.50 m.
- **Parents and grandparents:** the same rule applies at every age. Size by height, not by age.
- **Washing:** every Mbolster comes apart the same way, so one routine covers the whole household.

## Can a bolster help with neck or back pain?

A bolster can reduce strain by keeping you in a steadier side-sleeping position, but it will not resolve pain on its own. Neck comfort depends mostly on your head pillow. Lower-back comfort depends on your hips staying level, which a bolster between the knees helps with.

Older bolster articles, including some of our own, promised better circulation, easier breathing or pain relief. We have not found good evidence for those claims, so we no longer make them.

See a doctor or physiotherapist if your pain:

- does not settle, or keeps coming back
- comes with numbness, tingling or weakness in an arm or leg
- started after a fall, accident or other injury

## Can you use a bolster during pregnancy?

Yes. A bolster between the knees is one simple way to make side sleeping more comfortable as your bump grows. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stillbirth/prevention/) advises going to sleep on your side, left or right, from 28 weeks of pregnancy.
