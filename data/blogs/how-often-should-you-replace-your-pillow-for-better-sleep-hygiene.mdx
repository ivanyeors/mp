How often should you replace your pillow?

Most conventional pillows should be replaced every 1.5 to 2 years, depending on material, usage, and hygiene. 

Why is replacing your pillow important for sleep health?

Old pillows can:

Lose support and cause neck misalignment
Accumulate dust mites, sweat, and bacteria
Trigger allergies and skin irritation
Reduce sleep quality over time


What are signs that you should replace your pillow?

You may need a new pillow if:

Your pillow feels flat or lumpy
You wake up with neck or shoulder pain
Your pillow no longer supports your sleeping posture
There is a noticeable smell or discoloration


Do washable pillows last longer?

Yes. Pillows that are:

Fully washable
Made with modular fillings
Designed for hygienetend to last longer and stay fresher with proper care.


Can you extend pillow lifespan without compromising hygiene?

Yes, by choosing pillows that:

Have removable, washable components
Allow firmness adjustment instead of replacement
Use breathable materials suitable for Singapore’s climate
