Side sleeping is one of the most common and healthy sleep positions, especially for spinal alignment and digestion. However, without the right support, it can also lead to pressure points on the shoulders, hips, and knees—causing discomfort, misalignment, and even disrupted sleep. That’s where the Mbolster by Mpillow comes in.
Designed with side sleepers in mind, the Mbolster offers adjustable firmness and size options, making it a perfect fit for various body types and comfort preferences. Unlike conventional bolsters, the Mbolster is modular, meaning you can remove or add filling to suit your needs—whether you like a firmer support to hug or a softer bolster to tuck between your knees.
Here are a few key benefits of using Mbolster for side sleepers:

Spinal Alignment: By placing the Mbolster between your knees, it helps align your hips and spine, reducing strain on the lower back and minimizing morning stiffness.
Shoulder &amp; Hip Relief: Hugging the bolster reduces pressure on the shoulder and supports the upper arm, helping distribute your body weight evenly and preventing pain points.
Improved Blood Circulation: With better alignment and less pressure on your joints, you’re less likely to wake up due to numbness or poor circulation.
Custom Comfort: The adjustable design means you can tailor the bolster’s firmness and size to your sleep style—ensuring it’s not too bulky or too flat.
Hygienic &amp; Washable: Like the Mpillow, the Mbolster is fully washable inside and out, keeping your sleep environment fresh and clean.

If you’re a side sleeper searching for better rest and body support, the Mbolster might be your new bedtime essential.
