## What is tech neck?

Tech neck is a condition caused by long periods of looking down at screens, such as phones, laptops, and tablets. This posture places extra strain on the neck, shoulders, and upper spine, leading to stiffness, pain, and headaches.

## Why is tech neck common in Singapore?

Tech neck is increasingly common because:

- Many people work long hours at desks
- Frequent mobile phone use while commuting
- Poor posture while using laptops and tablets
- Lack of recovery during sleep

Without proper neck support at night, muscles remain tense and fail to recover.

## What symptoms does tech neck cause?

Common symptoms include:

- Neck and shoulder pain
- Headaches
- Stiffness when waking up
- Rounded shoulders
- Limited neck movement

## How can a pillow help relieve tech neck?

A pillow helps by:

- Supporting the natural cervical curve
- Keeping the neck aligned with the spine
- Reducing pressure on muscles and joints
- Allowing the neck to recover overnight

Without proper support, tech neck tension continues even during sleep.
